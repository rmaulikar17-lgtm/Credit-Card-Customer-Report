# Credit Card Customer Report | Power BI

This **Credit Card Customer Report** is a comprehensive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze customer spending behavior, revenue contribution, income patterns, and demographic trends. It helps financial institutions and business analysts understand how different customer segments generate revenue and use credit services.

---

## What this dashboard delivers

This dashboard provides a **data-driven view of credit card customer performance** by combining revenue, income, interest, and transaction data across customer demographics and time periods.

It enables users to:

- Track total revenue, income, and interest earned  
- Analyze spending behavior across age, gender, and income groups  
- Identify top-revenue generating customer segments  
- Monitor monthly and quarterly revenue trends  
- Support better credit and marketing decisions  

---

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard displays key financial and customer metrics such as:

- **Total Revenue**  
- **Total Interest**  
- **Total Income**  
- **Average Credit Score (CSS)**  

These KPIs provide a quick snapshot of portfolio performance.

---

## Key Visual Insights

### Revenue Trends
- **Revenue by Year, Quarter, Month, and Gender** shows how male and female customers contribute to revenue over time.

### Customer Demographics
- **Revenue by Age Group** identifies which age segments generate the highest income.  
- **Revenue by Marital Status** highlights spending differences between married and single customers.  
- **Revenue by Education Level** reveals how education impacts customer value.  

### Financial Segmentation
- **Revenue by Income Group** compares high, medium, and low income customers.  
- **Revenue by Dependent Count** shows how family size affects spending behavior.  

### Geographic & Job Insights
- **Revenue by Top 5 States** highlights the most profitable locations.  
- **Customer Job Analysis** reveals which professions contribute the most revenue, transactions, and income.

---

## Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes slicers for:
- **Quarter Selection (Q1–Q4)**  
- **Week Start Date**  
- **Income Group (Low, Medium, High)**  
- **Gender**

These allow users to drill down into specific time periods and customer segments.
